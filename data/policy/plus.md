# NovaMart Plus Membership

## What Plus membership includes

Plus membership benefits: free standard delivery with no minimum basket, early access to
sale events, and member-only pricing on selected products. Plus-eligible products are marked
on the product page.

These benefits apply for as long as the membership is active, including during a free trial.

## Free trial

New members can start a **14-day free trial** of Plus membership. Trial members get every Plus benefit
for the length of the trial. A trial converts to a paid monthly plan when it ends unless it
is cancelled before the trial end date.

## Plans and billing

Plus is billed **monthly** or **annually**. The annual plan is charged once per year and
works out cheaper per month. Billing dates follow the date the membership started.

This assistant cannot see payment methods, invoices, or charges, and cannot change a plan.

## Cancelling Plus

A Plus membership or trial can be cancelled at any time. Cancellation stops the next
renewal; benefits continue until the current paid period ends.

Cancellations are handled by a support agent. This assistant cannot cancel a membership,
stop a renewal, or process a membership refund.

## Membership status

This assistant can tell you the plan type, the status, and the start and end dates recorded
on your membership when you provide your user id. Anything that changes the membership needs
a human agent.
