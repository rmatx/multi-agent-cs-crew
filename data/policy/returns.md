# NovaMart Returns Policy

## Return window

Most NovaMart items can be returned within **14 days** of the order date. The window is
counted from the date the order was placed, not the date it arrived. Orders older than 14
days are outside the standard window and need a support agent to review.

Items that arrived damaged or faulty are outside this rule — report those at any time and an
agent will take over the case.

## Items that cannot be returned

Gift cards, digital downloads, and perishable grocery items are not returnable. Opened
personal-care and hygiene products cannot be returned once the seal is broken.

Everything else is returnable in its original condition, with all included accessories.

## How to start a return

Open the order in your NovaMart account and choose **Start a return**. Pick a reason, print
the prepaid label, and drop the parcel at any partner carrier point. You do not need to
contact support first for an in-window return.

Support cannot start, approve, or complete a return on your behalf in this chat.

## Return processing times

Once the warehouse receives a returned parcel, inspection normally takes **3 to 5 business
days**. Public holidays in your country can add to that, because the warehouse and the
carriers are closed on those days.

Processing time is an estimate, never a commitment. Nobody in this chat can tell you the
exact day a return will finish processing.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled by a human agent. This assistant cannot issue, approve, calculate, or
promise a refund, and it cannot tell you when money will arrive in your account.

Any question about refund amounts, refund timing, chargebacks, or payment methods is passed
to a support agent who can see the billing system.

## Exchanges

NovaMart does not process direct exchanges. Return the original item within the window and
place a new order for the replacement.
