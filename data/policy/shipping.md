# NovaMart Shipping Policy

## Delivery times and shipping speed

Standard delivery is **3 to 5 business days** after an order is placed. Express delivery is
**1 to 2 business days**. Plus members get free standard delivery on every order with no
minimum basket size.

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays in the destination country.

## Tracking an order

This assistant can tell you an order's **status** and the date it was placed. It cannot give
you a tracking number, a carrier name, or a live parcel location — NovaMart's support data
does not carry those fields, so no agent in this chat has them either.

For parcel-level tracking, use the carrier link in your shipping confirmation email.

## Delayed orders

An order that has not arrived within the delivery estimate is treated as delayed once the
estimate has passed by two business days. A support agent can open a case for a delayed
order; this assistant can confirm the order's status and dates so the agent starts with the
facts.

## Shipping addresses

Addresses cannot be changed after an order is placed. A support agent must review any
address problem, including a parcel sent to an old address.

## Shipping costs

Standard delivery is free on orders over 35.00 and otherwise costs 4.99. Express delivery
costs 9.99. Plus members pay nothing for standard delivery at any basket size.

Shipping charges already applied to an order cannot be adjusted in this chat.
